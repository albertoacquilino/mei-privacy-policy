# Privacy Policy for Music Education Interface

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy describes Music Education Interface (MEI), including iOS version 2.0.2. MEI is a noncommercial academic music education application.

## Information collected

Exercise records contain a device identifier, device model, platform and operating-system version; exercise settings and instrument selection; session start/end times, duration and completion status; and applicable numerical pitch and intonation results. Recorded durations describe exercise sessions rather than necessarily all time the app is open.

On iOS, the identifier is Apple's identifier for vendor. Records can be associated across sessions on the same device and are not anonymous merely because no name or email is requested. Sign-in and registration are not enabled in this version.

## Purposes and research outputs

We use device-linked exercise records for academic research on music learning, exercise performance and application usage, and to improve the software. Device metadata may also be used to investigate compatibility issues and understand differences in application behavior across devices. Research may result in scientific publications.

Public research outputs contain only aggregated or appropriately anonymized results, without device identifiers. Any exception required by a particular approved research protocol requires separate review before it is implemented.

We do not use these data for advertising, share them with data brokers, or combine them with external datasets for advertising or tracking.

## Processing and storage

Microphone audio is analyzed on the device. The exercise pipeline does not upload raw microphone audio; numerical exercise results may be sent to Firebase.

Preferences and pending exercise records are stored locally. Pending records may be retried for upload on later use. We use Google Firebase/Cloud Firestore to process and store exercise records. See [Firebase's privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

The app also loads Google's Identity Services client library, although sign-in is not enabled in this version. Its network requests are handled by Google; see [Google Identity Services](https://developers.google.com/identity/gsi/web/guides/overview) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). This application should not be understood as involving only local processing or no third-party requests.

## Retention

Our retention target for identifiable device-linked exercise records is 12 months, subject to applicable institutional research-data retention requirements. We are establishing an administrative review and request-handling procedure. Automated retention enforcement is not implemented, and a complete deletion workflow has not been operationally verified. Deletion is not guaranteed to occur on an exact date.

Any applicable study-specific retention requirement must be documented and communicated where relevant. Appropriately anonymized or aggregated research findings may be kept longer, including in scientific publications.

## Privacy and deletion requests

Alberto Acquilino is the responsible contact for privacy and deletion requests at [albertoacquilino@gmail.com](mailto:albertoacquilino@gmail.com).

We aim to respond within 30 days. This is a response target, not a guarantee that every record can always be identified or deleted within that period.

Exercise records are linked to device identifiers rather than email addresses. We will work with requesters to locate their records and explain identification limitations or applicable institutional retention requirements. An email address alone may not identify the relevant records.

Deleting server records does not automatically remove local app data or pending uploads from a device. We will explain any additional steps needed when handling a request. Uninstalling the app does not delete previously uploaded records.
